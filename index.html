An independent studio, by design

A practice of patience, proportion, and the personal.

For more than three decades, we have designed homes that are quietly extraordinary — never louder than the lives within them.

Shamains Design Group is an interior architecture and design studio in Defence Colony, New Delhi, founded by Shamain Singh. We are trusted for high-end residential work across India and Dubai, and for interiors that feel collected rather than decorated.

Every commission begins with listening — to the site, to the light, to the rituals of the people who will call the space home. From there, we draw on a deep respect for craft, material honesty and the kind of detail you notice only when it is missing.

We are equally at ease with a restored family home and a contemporary villa. What connects the work is restraint, warmth, and an instinct for how a room should make you feel.

04 Our Approach Four movements, one home

A process measured
in listening.

Good design cannot be rushed. We work at a deliberate pace, with a limited number of commissions each year, so that every decision is given the time it deserves.

  1. 01

    Listen

    We begin with how you live — the rituals, the collections, the rooms you always gravitate toward. Then we study the site, the light, and what the architecture is quietly asking for.

  2. 02

    Imagine

    Concepts emerge as a story: plans, mood, material studies and hand sketches. We present a clear point of view, then refine it with you until the home feels unmistakably yours.

  3. 03

    Refine

    This is where the work becomes precise — joinery details, stone edges, the temperature of a light. We prototype, sample and edit, because restraint is a discipline of many small choices.

  4. 04

    Realise

    Our studio stays on site with trusted craftspeople through the build and the final styling. The result should feel inevitable — as though it has always been there.

“The best rooms are the ones that make you feel like you already knew them.”
